# 外联模板（第 2 版）

结构：一句自我介绍；一句背书（产品质量、Jane Street 和 Citadel 从业者支持）；其余是合作意向、了解需求、高质量和有竞争力的价格。

每家只改这些方括号：[Company] / [公司名]、[Name] / [称呼]、[Hook] / [合作句]。
[Hook] / [合作句] 是一句：对方最近做的一件事，加上我们想在哪方面合作。
以下保持原样，由发件人填：[Your name]、[姓名]、微信/电话：[ ]、[介绍人]。

## 海外邮件（英文）

Subject: Expert data for [Company]'s post-training

Hi [Name],

I'm [Your name], co-founder of SimReal (simreal.co), where we build RL environments, verifiers and expert data for post-training.

Professionals from Jane Street and Citadel support our work, and every environment is red-teamed before release: 400 reward-hacking attempts so far, none successful.

[Hook] I'd like to understand where you're short: which domains, which formats (SFT, preference data, rubrics, RL tasks, evals), and what volume and turnaround you need. We can start with a small paid pilot: high-quality data at a competitive price.

Open to a 20-minute call next week?

Best,
[Your name]
Co-founder, SimReal
